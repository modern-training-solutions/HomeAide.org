About

Help people find home health help — without selling them anything

HomeAide.org is a non-revenue public resource hosted by Modern Training Solutions. We are not a nonprofit organization, a home health agency, or a paid referral network. We publish Medicare’s own directory of certified home health agencies so a daughter in a parking lot, or a spouse at the kitchen table, can find a phone number and a quality snapshot in one sitting.

The problem we are trying to shrink

Home health is one of the few ways older adults and people recovering from illness can stay at home with skilled support. The official data already exists. It is just hard to use: wide spreadsheets, clinical measure names, and no story around the decision. Families mix up home health (usually Medicare skilled care) with home care (private-duty help with daily life). That mix-up costs time, money, and sometimes a safe discharge.

What this site does today

  • Loads every provider in the CMS Home Health Care Agencies dataset (12,460 agencies in the July 2026 release).
  • Shows contact information, ownership type, listed services, star ratings, and outcome measures.
  • Publishes practical guides on coverage, ratings, and the first phone call.

What this site does not do

  • We do not place aides, take commissions, or sell leads.
  • We do not claim nonprofit status.
  • We do not replace Medicare Care Compare or your clinician’s discharge plan.

Who hosts it

Modern Training Solutions hosts HomeAide as a community resource related to its work in health education. The site is designed to generate no revenue: no ads, no membership wall, no “sponsored” agencies.
