Methodology

Where the agency list comes from

The directory is the CMS Provider Data Catalog dataset Home Health Care Agencies (identifier 6jpm-sxkc), July 2026 release. CMS describes it as a list of all home health agencies registered with Medicare, including addresses, phone numbers, and quality measure ratings.

  • Source: data.cms.gov/provider-data/dataset/6jpm-sxkc
  • Topic page: Home health services
  • Last modified in source metadata: May 27, 2026 · released July 15, 2026 · planned update October 21, 2026
  • Local copy: data/HH_Provider_Jul2026.csv (12,460 agencies, 96 columns)

What we display

Name, CMS Certification Number, address, phone, ownership, certification date, six service flags (nursing, PT, OT, speech, medical social work, home health aide), quality-of-patient-care star rating, selected process/outcome percentages, national-rate comparison categories, and Medicare spending relative to the national average.

What we do not display yet

HHCAHPS patient-survey detail, zip-code service-area files, websites, hours, languages, waitlists, and staff bios. Those are the enrichment tasks described in the project README for the business manager.

Refresh cadence

Replace the CSV from CMS, rerun the build notes in README.md, and republish data/. Do not rewrite ratings by hand.
