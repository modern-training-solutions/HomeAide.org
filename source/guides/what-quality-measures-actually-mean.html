What those quality percentages on an agency profile actually mean

“Patients got better at walking” is an outcome rate, not a guarantee about your parent.

HomeAide editors · Updated October 2, 2026 · 8 min read

Key takeaways

  • Percentages are agency-level rates from CMS reporting periods, with footnotes when data are thin.
  • National-rate categories (better / same / worse) exist for discharge to community and preventable hospital use.
  • Spending relative to national average is not a quality grade by itself.

Read three numbers together

Timely start of care tells you about operations. Walking and bathing improvement tell you about functional outcomes. Hospitalization and discharge-to-community categories tell you about how often people bounce back to the hospital. Together they are a sketch, not a verdict.
