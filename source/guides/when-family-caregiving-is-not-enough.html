When family caregiving is not enough — and how to say so

Burnout is a clinical risk, not a character flaw. Home health is one of the on-ramps to sharing the work.

HomeAide editors · Updated October 2, 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Night-time wandering, repeated falls, and missed medications are signals to add skilled help.
  • You can bring in a Medicare agency without “giving up” as a family.
  • Search for agencies that list both nursing and home health aide services if personal care is part of the gap.

A script

“We can keep evenings and weekends. We need a nurse to look at the wound and an aide for mornings so nobody is doing this at 5 a.m. after a full job.” That sentence is enough for a discharge planner or a primary-care office to start a home health referral.
