12 questions to ask a home health agency on the first call

A good first call is short and specific. These questions surface staffing, aide availability, and how they handle nights.

HomeAide editors · Updated October 2, 2026 · 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • Ask whether they serve your ZIP this week, not whether they are “in the county.”
  • Ask who shows up for start of care and whether an aide can be scheduled after the nurse assessment.
  • Write down the name of the intake person.

The list

  1. Do you accept Medicare patients at [ZIP] right now?
  2. When is the earliest start-of-care visit?
  3. Which skilled services are you actually staffing — nursing, PT, OT, speech, aide?
  4. How do you cover call-outs?
  5. Who is the after-hours number?
  6. Do clinicians speak [language]?
  7. How do you coordinate with our physician?
  8. What would make you unable to take this case?
  9. How do you handle wound photos / portal access?
  10. If skilled care ends, do you offer private-duty, or do we need another company?
  11. What should we have ready in the home for the first visit?
  12. What is your CMS Certification Number so we can look you up?

That last question is how you land on the matching HomeAide profile and Medicare Care Compare page.
