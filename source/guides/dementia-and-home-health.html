Dementia at home: what Medicare home health can and cannot do

Home health can stabilize a medical issue in someone living with dementia. It is not a memory-care program.

HomeAide editors · Updated October 2, 2026 · 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • A new infection, fall, or medication change can open a home health episode even when dementia is the bigger life problem.
  • Ask how the agency trains aides on communication and wandering.
  • Plan for private-duty or adult day services if supervision is the real need.

Use the medical door

Medicare home health still needs a skilled need. Families often get help after a UTI, a hip fracture, or a hospital delirium episode. While the nurse and therapist are in the home, ask them to coach the family on cues, lighting, and a calmer bathing routine.
