Finding agencies that actually list home health aide services

Not every Medicare home health agency offers aide services. The CMS file includes a yes/no flag — and we surface it.

HomeAide editors · Updated October 2, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • On HomeAide, use the “Lists home health aide services” filter.
  • About three in four agencies in the July 2026 file mark home health aide services as offered.
  • Offered in the file still means you must confirm staffing this week.

Why the flag exists

CMS records whether the agency offers nursing, therapies, medical social services, and home health aide services. Families looking for bathing help should start with agencies marked yes, then still ask intake if an aide can be scheduled after the start-of-care visit.
