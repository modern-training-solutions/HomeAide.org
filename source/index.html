Why HomeAide exists

People deserve a clear path to in-home help

After a fall, a hospital stay, or a slow decline, families are asked to choose a home health agency in a hurry. Medicare already publishes who is certified and how they score. We put that directory in one calm place, written for families — not for billing departments.
